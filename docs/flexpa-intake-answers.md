## Business Need

### What does your business do, and who do you serve?

Quarterback Health is a consumer healthcare platform for adults managing care for themselves and their families. We help users coordinate appointments, organize records, and understand their coverage.

### What's the problem you need Flexpa to solve, how are you handling it today, and how is it impacting your business?

We need first-party claims and EOB data to help users understand their coverage and identify reimbursement and appeal opportunities. Today we work from manual document uploads and transaction-based estimates, which is not reliable. It's the largest unmet need in our product.

### What is your budget for solving this problem?

Flexible based on structure. Happy to discuss on the call.

### Do you have a timeline to solve this problem?

Active priority. Targeting Q3 2026 for live integration.

### Who are the key people involved in making a buying decision (business, technical, others)?

Founder and technical lead. Small team, fast decisions, no procurement layer.

### Anything else we should know before the call?

We're authenticated, live with users, and ready to engage.
